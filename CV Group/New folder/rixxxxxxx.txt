🛠️ Simple Studio Setup (Minimalist & Professional)

You don't need a professional studio. A clean, "tech-focused" home office vibe works best for developers.
1. Lighting (The Most Important Part)

    The Window Trick: Face a window directly. Natural light is the most flattering.

    Avoid Backlighting: Never have a window behind you, or you will look like a silhouette.

    The "Ring Light" Alternative: If you’re filming at night, place a desk lamp behind your monitor so it bounces off the wall and onto your face.

2. Audio (Don't use the Laptop Mic)

    Use your Headset: If you have a decent set of headphones with a mic (like AirPods or a wired headset), use those. They are much better at filtering out echo than your laptop's built-in mic.

    Silence the room: Turn off the AC/Fan for the 5 minutes you are recording to remove background hum.

3. Framing & Background

    Background: A bookshelf, a clean wall, or your workstation with your monitors showing some code or the Rixxa dashboard. This subconsciously proves you are an active developer.

    Eye Level: Stack books under your laptop/camera so you are looking directly into the lens, not down at the screen.

💡 Pro-Tips for Upwork

    Show, Don't Just Tell: While you are talking about "Rixxa" or "Facial Recognition," use a simple video editor to overlay a 2-second screen recording of the project over your face.

    Dress the Part: Wear what you’d normally wear to a client meeting (usually a clean t-shirt or polo).

    The Thumbnail: Upwork lets you pick a thumbnail. Smile and make sure it’s a high-quality, bright image so people actually click "Play."
